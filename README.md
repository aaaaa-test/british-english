# KJV Words and Bible

Every word of the King James Bible, learnt through its verses. The app also covers Bible reading and study.

**Open the app:** https://aaaaa-test.github.io/kjv-words/

It works on phones and computers. You can add it to your home screen, and it keeps working offline.

## What it does

- **Words:** every word form in the KJV, with meanings and optional Korean. You can choose words by level, kind,
  frequency, Bible range and more, combining filters with AND or OR.
- **Reading:** read by book, chapter and verse, with the 1611 first edition beside the 1769 text.
- **The Bible in order:** go through the Bible a passage at a time or one verse at a time. Each word is learnt once, the
  first time it appears.
- **Study:** memory verses, a reading plan, lessons on KJV English, and a study record by date, by passage and on a calendar.

For how the app is built, see `CLAUDE.md` and `tools/README.md`.
